Our Mission:

Provide enrichment for kids and adults through D&D by offering opportunities to wrestle with important social and ethical dilemmas through the safety of abstraction.

What do you mean by D&D?

Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) is part of a family of games known as Table-Top Roleplaying Games (TTRPG). D&D is officially owned by Wizards of the Coast and enabled for free world-wide use by the Open Gaming License. When we use the term D&D, we use it for popular recognition and shorthand for our custom TTRPG game or other published TTRPG games. Think of 5.5E D&D as the engine that our games run on, and then we build the rest of the world and mechanics based on our experience and who shows up to the table.

Who benefits from this?

Children and adults both benefit from D&D, developing/practicing skills such as ...

  • Creative Writing
  • Conflict Resolution
  • Team Building
  • Problem Solving
  • Personal Identity Exploration
  • Setting/Upholding Boundaries
  • Active Listening
  • Gameplay Comprehension
  • Mental Math
